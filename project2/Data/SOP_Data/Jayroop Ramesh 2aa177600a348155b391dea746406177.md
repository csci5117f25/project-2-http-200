**Additional Advice**

Bear in mind it’s applicable for UK/EU only since I did not apply to US or Canada. I also applied after 2 years of Masters/Research Experience, so if you are applying straight from undergrad, subtract each category of paper requirements by 2 for the minimum bound.
1) Short list professors in your primary area of interest or research experience. From browsing your profile just now, I assume it’s in the thriving area of smart tech in education. I suggest having at least 30 professors and their contact emails in a list along with a 3 line summary of their most recent publications and research material. Then email them one by one with a general messaging asking if they’d like a PhD student and attach your CV.
2) In your statement of purpose highlight both your previous research learnings and how it connects to the professor/university’s research that you are targeting. If you can find the current PhD students in that particular field, follow their LinkedIn to see their achievements to set your PhD plan accordingly.
3) Have a 2 page CV with experience, publications and achievements. Simple format. GPA of 3.5+ is minimum, 3.7+ is good, 3.9+ is best. GRE can help if you have taken it, but no need to take it additionally if UK.
4) If top-tier pubs (NeurIPs, CVPR, ICML etc), 1-3 is enough. If Q1/Q2 subject journals (Springer, IEEE, Frontiers, Wiley, BMC), 4-5 recommended. Average IEEE/ACM conferences only, 5-7 is a good reference. AVOID WASET, OR NON SCOPUS/SCI/WoS indexed venues!!
Good luck, and wish you all the best!  :D
