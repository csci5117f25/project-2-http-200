**Additional Advice**

I have been an average student all my life, attended an average high school, did my undergrad from a completely unrecognizable college, grew up with average friends and with average dreams. My life did not change when I got admitted for a PhD at some very reputable institutes, it changed when i believed that i could achieve it. Each time i submitted an application, i felt like i would be rejected as my undergrad college was very bad, i did not have supercool internships or for that matter, my publications too were not very prestigious. However, eventually i got admits from the New York University, University of Pittsburgh, Arizona State University, National University of Singapore (ranks 2nd worldwide in CS) and Deakin University at Australia.
The point is, if you think your profile is not good enough, its okay, try your best, and unexpected opportunities will come to you to improve yourself. There will be always better profiles than yours, you can't help that, but you can work on trying to improve on yours, so do that relentlessly.
