**Additional Advice**

I was super fortunate to receive CS PhD offers from CMU (LTI), HKU, Stanford, UIUC, and UW, and my experience makes me feel that the SoP is actually also important for CS PhD applications (besides LOR), especially at top schools. My advice for SoP writing is: (1) There are many great online resources for CS PhD SoP writing and please read them! (2) Focus on *what you want to do in the future*, with what you have done so far supporting that, but not being the main point; (3) Get feedback from as many people as you can, and encourage them to give you tough & honest critiques; and (4) Start early so you have plenty of time to revise your draft.
