**Additional Advice**

Your interests may be really broad/varied right now, and that's okay. However, I think I leaned into it a little bit too much by grounding my SOP in three fairly distinct research questions, which I didn't change very much institution by institution. If I were to do it over again, I think I would've tailored my SOPs a little more to each institution and been more clear about previous interests vs. what I really, actually wanted to do moving forward.

This is with hindsight, though. I don't think I would've have had the capacity to write several different SOPs. I also think writing it the way I did allowed me to use my interviews as a tool to figure out what I actually wanted to do. And I will say -- my current advisor told me in my interview that my SOP was really solid, so maybe take all of this with a grain of salt.
