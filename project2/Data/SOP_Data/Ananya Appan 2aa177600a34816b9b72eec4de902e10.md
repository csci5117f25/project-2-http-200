**Additional Advice**

1. Mail professors before applying (if they encourage you to do so in their website) and request for a call if they seem interested. If you have not met the professor before, this could help you understand if you can get along with them. It could also help you shortlist universities.
2. Look for professors of all ranks. Assistant / Associate professors with fewer students may have more time and enthusiasm. Full professors may have more experience and a better understanding of how the field has grown over the years.
